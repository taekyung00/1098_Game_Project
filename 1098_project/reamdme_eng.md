**-♗Explanation of what the game is and how to play♗-**

"10..9..8.." is a dungeon exploration game where you can only move 10 steps per room.
Avoid or defeat the chess pieces and eventually take down the Chess King!
(The king isn’t implemented yet... but he’s coming for you.)

**-features-**

-The number above your character shows how many moves you have left in the current room.
-Every time you move, enemies will chase after you.
-Not a fan of chess pieces?
Don’t worry — you can preview their next move on your turn

**-Control-**

W - Up
A - Left
S - Down
D - Right

-If an enemy is close enough, pressing the direction key will attack instead of move.
Whether you choose to fight or avoid is up to you.

-If your attack hits and defeats a chess piece,
you’ll be rewarded with extra moves!

-Reach the stairs to escape each room.

Good Luck.
